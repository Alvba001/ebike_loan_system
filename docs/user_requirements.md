# User Requirements

## Borrower (Student)
- The Borrower shall be able to register for an account using their name, email, and password.
- The Borrower shall be able to log in to the system.
- The Borrower shall be able to view their dashboard with loan status and repayment information.
- The Borrower shall be able to browse available electric bike models.
- The Borrower shall be able to apply for a loan by selecting a bike, loan amount, and duration.
- The Borrower shall be able to upload required documents (ID Card, Utility Bill) during the loan application.
- The Borrower shall be able to view the status of their loan application (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
- The Borrower shall be able to view their repayment schedule.
- The Borrower shall be able to make loan repayments.
- The Borrower shall be able to view their repayment history.

## Admin
- The Admin shall be able to log in to the administrative dashboard.
- The Admin shall be able to add new electric bikes to the system inventory (Serial Number, Model).
- The Admin shall be able to view a list of all loan applications.
- The Admin shall be able to view detailed information for a specific loan application, including uploaded documents.
- The Admin shall be able to approve a loan application.
- The Admin shall be able to reject a loan application.
- The Admin shall be able to view all repayments made by borrowers.
- The Admin shall be able to generate reports on loans and repayments.
- The Admin shall be able to check for overdue loan repayments (Reminders).
